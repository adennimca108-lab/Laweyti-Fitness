Workout Programs

💪 Strength Training

Build strength and muscle with a balanced routine.

  • Squats — 3 × 12
  • Push-ups — 3 × 10
  • Lunges — 3 × 10
  • Plank — 3 × 30 sec

🏃 Cardio

Improve endurance and cardiovascular fitness.

  • Warm-up — 5 min
  • Jogging — 15 min
